# iMeditate

A mobile app for meditating on scripture, for children ages 4 to 17, their parents, and any adult.

iMeditate helps children — and anyone — meditate on scripture using three steps drawn from the Hebrew word *hagar*: **Ponder, Mutter, and Roar**. A child sees a scripture, pictures themselves inside it, says it quietly and repeatedly, then declares it aloud with confidence and joy. Along the way, a **Soldier companion** grows in rank as the child completes more scriptures.

This repo currently holds **Version 1: The Small First Version** — the smallest version worth putting in front of real families.

## The one thing V1 must prove

> A child can complete the three steps on a scripture, enjoy it, want to come back, and feel proud watching their Soldier grow.

## How it works

1. **Parent signs up** and adds one child (first name/nickname + age group only).
2. **Child meets the Soldier** at his first rank (Recruit) and learns the three steps.
3. **Home** shows the Soldier, today's suggested scripture with a big Start button, and a way to browse the theme.
4. **Child completes the three steps:**
   - **Ponder:** Scripture is read aloud. Guiding questions appear one at a time ("Where are you in this scene?", "What do you see, hear, and feel?"). Child writes or draws what they imagined. No timer — tap "I'm done" when ready.
   - **Mutter:** Scripture appears one phrase at a time. Child says each phrase quietly. Counter tracks repetitions (goal: 20x). Soft background sound plays. No microphone, nothing recorded.
   - **Roar:** Child declares the scripture loudly with confidence and joy. Uplifting music plays, words light up as they go. Boldest, most energetic moment in the app.
5. **Celebrate:** Rank-up celebration if the Soldier is promoted, otherwise a warm message from the Soldier.

**Focus rule:** If a child leaves for more than 5 minutes, they restart from Ponder (with a gentle warning before time runs out). Phone calls pause the clock. Anything written/drawn in Ponder is kept.

## Wording by age group

| Age group | Experience |
|---|---|
| 4–7 | Very simple words. Everything read aloud. Playful Soldier. |
| 8–11 | Simple wording. Reading + listening offered. |
| 12–17 | More grown-up wording. Heroic Soldier, non-cartoon story. |

## The Soldier

The child's companion and face of the brand (including the app icon). Starts at **Recruit**, rises through **Private → Sergeant → Captain** in V1 as the child completes new scriptures. Armies gather under him as he rises. Tone is always adventurous and joyful, never violent.

## V1 Scope

**Included:**
- Adult sign-up + one child profile (name, age group, Soldier)
- One free theme: **Sound Mind** (3 scriptures)
- Ponder → Mutter → Roar, unlocked in order
- Soldier + first ranks
- Calm, bold look: deep blues and reds
- Safety basics: no ads, no messaging, minimal data, easy deletion

**Explicitly out of V1** (deferred to full plan):
- Payments / subscriptions, jewel shop / jewels
- Streaks, badges, reminders
- Full parent progress view
- Messages from Pastor Chris Oyakhilome
- More themes / later ranks (General, stars, cities, continents, regions)
- Multiple children / parent personal plan

## Parent role (V1)

- Creates account, adds child
- Sees simple setup confirmation
- PIN-gated parent area
- Can delete child profile + all data anytime
- Can also meditate simply with the same Sound Mind theme (separate personal plan comes later)

## Safety and trust

- No ads, ever. No chatting/messaging.
- Minimal data: first name/nickname + age group only. No photos, location, or school.
- Microphone never used.
- Private storage. Parent can delete child data anytime.
- Children never see prices or buy buttons.

## Look and feel

Bold and adventurous — deep blues and reds, lots of energy. Quieter and steadier on home / Ponder / Mutter, bolder and livelier for Roar and rank-up celebration. Store name: **iMeditate**.
