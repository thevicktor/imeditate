DESIGN SYSTEM · V1

iMeditate

A meditation companion for children and adults, built around three steps drawn from the Hebrew word hagar: ponder, mutter, and roar. Bold and adventurous, deep blue and red, with a Soldier who grows alongside every person who uses it.

FOUNDATION
Colour

Six colours, each with one job. Gold is reserved for moments that are earned — it never appears as ordinary interface chrome.

Deep navy#0F1E3D

Base ground. Home, Ponder, Mutter, app chrome.

Signal red#B3202A

Roar screen and celebration only. Never an action.

Ember Amber#D9772E

Every primary action — Start, Continue, Subscribe, Declare it.

Ember gold#F4B942

Rank, jewels, badges — earned only.

Parchment#F7F4EC

Ponder's writing surface. Warmth, not glare.

Dusk blue#8FA6C9

Secondary text, quiet-state UI.

Growth green#1E7D5C

Streaks and progress — distinct from reward gold.

FOUNDATION
Typography

Two families. Fraunces carries the brand voice — headlines, the Soldier's story, celebration moments. Inter carries everything read for information: scripture, questions, and interface text, legible at every age from 4 to 17.

DISPLAY — FRAUNCES
Roar it out

Used for headlines, the Soldier's rank, and celebration screens. Never for scripture text.

BODY — INTER
Be strong and courageous

Used for scripture, guiding questions, and all interface labels. Plain and legible, never decorated.

FOUNDATION
Adapting across ages 4–17

The token system never changes. What flexes is wording and the Soldier's illustration style — never layout, colour, or interaction pattern.

AGES 4–7

Very simple words

Everything read aloud. Soldier is round, friendly, exaggerated.

AGES 8–11

Simple wording

Reading and listening both offered. Soldier is proportioned, still warm.

AGES 12–17

Grown-up wording

Soldier is heroic, painterly — no cartoon feel.

FOUNDATION
Principles

01

Earned colour stays earned

Gold and the Soldier's insignia appear only at real achievement — never as default chrome — or the reward stops meaning anything.

02

The palette narrows as focus deepens

Home is navy and gold. Ponder strips to near-monochrome parchment for stillness. Roar is the only screen allowed to go loud.

03

The Soldier is illustrated, not iconographic

He is a character, not a logo — so he carries the age shift from playful to heroic through art alone, while the UI stays age-agnostic.

04

Scripture text is never decorated

No colour washes, no fancy framing. It is the one visual constant a 5-year-old and a 17-year-old both read the same way.
